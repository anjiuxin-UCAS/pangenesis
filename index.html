Organ Biological Age & Life Index

人体内存在多个以不同速率衰老的「时钟」。通过 403 项生物标志物构建 9 大器官系统的生物年龄,让衰老与健康状态可量化、可干预。

The human body holds multiple "clocks" aging at different speeds. Using 403 biomarkers, we construct biological ages for 9 organ systems — making aging measurable and actionable.
